TEST MATRIX

What changed between runs

Initial virtual cash $1/$3/$10; costs 1×/2×/5×; thresholds 0.7/1/1.4 in history and 0.8/1/1.35 in synthetic; holding periods 3/8/16 market candles (history) or 3/8 (synthetic); maker queue fill assumptions 15%/50%; market-order slippage 2/12 basis points. Six synthetic regimes: uptrend, downtrend, sideways, mean reverting, shock, and pump-and-dump.

Coinbase fee tier used in replay: 0.25% maker and 0.50% taker per side (reported by connected read-only query). No executable bid/ask spread or queue position was observed.

SOURCE BOUNDARIES

Methods, not stolen code

Market-making assumptions are informed by Poly Maker and HFTBacktest. The learning-router separation relates to River. Their real trading engines were not executed. The passive strategy is a limited-entry proxy, not a two-sided market maker.
