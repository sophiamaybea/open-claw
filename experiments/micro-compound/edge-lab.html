What the research actually establishes

We retrieved over 6,100 Coinbase daily candles for six markets during 2024–2026, plus earlier daily BTC/ETH/SOL history spanning 2021–2023. Candidate strategies were ranked on 2024 training and 2025 validation. The selected rules were also examined on 2026 and earlier years. This is an exploratory search with multiple-testing bias, simplified daily opening/closing prices, no bid/ask depth, no real order routing, and historically constant fee assumptions based on the account's current tier. This is not an audited trading system or expected return forecast.
